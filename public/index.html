How to Play

01 · CREATE / JOIN

Enter a nickname. Create a room or enter a 4-character room code.

02 · MOVE

PC: WASD or Arrow Keys. Mobile: drag on the arena to steer.

03 · GRAB + COMBO

Energy = 1, rare gold = 3, volatile red = 5, purple = 2×, void = −2. Chain pickups to build a combo multiplier.

04 · SURVIVE

Stay mobile, avoid rivals when needed, and use the arena edges strategically. There is no shrinking zone, so the full arena remains available.

05 · POWER-UPS

Collect dash, shield, magnet, overdrive, or phase boosts. Effects last briefly.

06 · BOUNTY

The current leader is marked ◆. Grab orange bounty energy for bonus points and challenge the leader.

🔊 Sound effects play automatically after your first interaction. Chase high-value targets, manage your power-ups, and use the full arena intelligently.
OrBlitz // ARENA RULES

Rules & Tactics

The round lasts 60 seconds. Score more than everyone else while managing risk, power-ups, combos and the full arena.

01 · OBJECTIVE

Move through the arena and collect targets. The player with the highest final score wins.

02 · TARGETS

🔵 Energy +1   🟡 Rare +3   🔴 Volatile +5   🟣 Multiplier gives 2× scoring briefly   ⚫ Void −2   🟠 Bounty +5.

03 · COMBOS

Collect targets quickly to build your combo. Higher combo levels increase your multiplier up to ×4. Losing your chain resets the combo.

05 · POWER-UPS

⚡ Dash = burst movement · 🛡 Shield = protection · 🧲 Magnet = pulls targets toward you · 🔥 Overdrive = speed boost · 👻 Phase = temporary hazard/player protection.

06 · BOUNTY

The current leader receives a ◆ BOUNTY marker. Bounty targets appear to create a reason to challenge the leader instead of simply avoiding everyone.

07 · CONTROLS

Desktop: WASD or Arrow Keys. Mobile: touch and drag on the arena in the direction you want to move. The game is real-time, so short controlled movements are more precise.

08 · PLAY SMART

Prioritize high-value targets, save defensive power-ups for risky moments, avoid chasing a low-value orb into a crowded fight, and use your rocket trail to make fast direction changes.

09 · WIN CONDITION

When the 60-second timer reaches zero, scoring stops and the final scoreboard determines the winner.

PRO TIP // Your player is a neon rocket. Use controlled bursts, preserve your combo, and curve around opponents instead of running directly through them.
